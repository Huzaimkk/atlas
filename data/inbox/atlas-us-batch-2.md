## ⚠️ The finding that outranks every row below

Five top US ECE programmes are now verified, and their English requirements split cleanly:

| Programme | Duolingo accepted? | Alternative | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rice** | ✅ **115** — profile clears with margin | TOEFL 90 / IELTS 7 | ~21 Dec |
| **UCSD** | ✅ **120** — profile is *exactly* at the line | IELTS 7 / PTE 65 | 16 Dec |
| **UC Berkeley** | ❌ Not accepted | TOEFL / IELTS | **1 Dec** |
| **UCLA** | ❌ Not accepted | TOEFL 87 / **IELTS 7.0** | 15 Dec |
| **Stanford** | Not published on admissions pages — assume TOEFL/IELTS | — | **1 Dec** |

**One test unlocks everything: IELTS 7.0.** It satisfies all five. Duolingo satisfies two.

A Duolingo 120 maps to roughly IELTS 7.0–7.5, so the ability is almost certainly already there — what's missing is the accepted certificate.

**The timing is the problem.** Berkeley and Stanford both close **1 December — 46 days**. IELTS typically needs 2–4 weeks to find a test slot and ~13 days for results. Booking this week makes it; booking in November does not.

**Gap Queue consequence:** "IELTS/TOEFL" is not a generic gap. It is a *dated* gap worth three top-five programmes, and it expires on 1 December.
