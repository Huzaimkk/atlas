### 2.1 Anthropic Fellows Program — 🟢 OPEN (rolling) ✅
- **What:** 4-month, full-time funded research with Anthropic mentors; goal is a public output (paper, benchmark, tool). Workstreams include AI Safety & Security, ML Systems & RL, and Economics & Policy (Anthropic Institute).
- **Next cohort:** expected to start **January 2027**; applications reviewed on a rolling basis.
- **Funding:** weekly stipend **3,850 USD / 2,310 GBP / 4,300 CAD**, ~**$15k/month compute**, close mentorship.
- **Eligibility:** No PhD or published papers required; strong Python and research execution matter. ⚠️ Must have **work authorization in the US, UK, or Canada** and be located there — **no visa sponsorship**.
- **Outcomes:** >40% of the first cohort joined Anthropic full-time.
- **Sources:** https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183053008 · https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183051008 · https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/

## 3. 🎓 FULLY FUNDED MASTER'S & PhD SCHOLARSHIPS

### 3.1 Chevening Scholarships 2027–28 (UK) — 🔴 closes 6 Oct 2026, 11:00 UTC ✅
- **Level:** One-year taught master's in the UK.
- **Timeline:** Opened 4 Aug 2026 → deadline **6 Oct 2026 11:00 UTC** → results June 2027 → submit unconditional UK offer by **8 July 2027** → start Sept/Oct 2027.
- **Requirements (commonly cited):** apply to three eligible UK courses; ~2,800 hours of work experience; return home for 2 years afterward.
- **Stipend (aggregator figures):** ~£1,378/mo outside London, ~£1,690/mo in London.
- **Sources:** https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/ · https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/scholarships-funding/chevening-scholarships

### 3.2 Knight-Hennessy Scholars 2027 (Stanford, USA) — 🔴 closes 6 Oct 2026, 1:00 pm PT ✅
- **Level:** Any full-time Stanford graduate degree (MS, PhD, MBA, JD, MD, etc.).
- **Two applications required:** (1) KHS by **6 Oct 2026**; (2) the Stanford degree application by the program deadline **or 1 Dec 2026, whichever is earlier**.
- **Covers:** tuition, living stipend, travel and relocation stipends; up to ~3 years (varies by degree). No citizenship or field restriction.
- **Sources:** https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission · https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/application-deadlines

### 3.3 Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027/28 (UK) — 🟢 OPEN ✅
- **Level:** Any full-time postgraduate course at Cambridge (PhD, MPhil, MSc, LLM, etc.). Any non-UK citizen.
- **Deadlines:** US citizens resident in the US → **14 Oct 2026**. All others → **8 Dec 2026 or 6 Jan 2027** depending on the course funding deadline (check the Cambridge Course Directory).
- **How:** No separate form — apply via the Cambridge Graduate Application Portal and tick Gates Cambridge in the funding section; includes an extra "Gates reference."
- **Covers:** full fees, maintenance (£22,050 for 12 months at 2025–26 rate), airfare, visa + IHS costs. ~80 awards/year, ~⅔ to PhDs.
- **Sources:** https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/timeline/ · https://www.scholars4dev.com/2043/gates-scholarships-for-international-students-at-university-of-cambridge/

### 3.4 Commonwealth Master's & PhD Scholarships 2027/28 (UK) — 🟢 closes 20 Oct 2026, 16:00 BST ✅
- **Who:** Citizens/residents of eligible low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries (Pakistan is a Commonwealth member) who couldn't otherwise afford UK study.
- **⚠️ Key detail:** You usually apply **through a national nominating body**, which sets its **own earlier deadline**. Missing the nominator's date means you're out, even if CSC's deadline hasn't passed.
- **Start:** Sept/Oct 2027. Master's = 1 year; PhD = up to 4 years.
- **Sources:** https://www.scholars4dev.com/4900/commonwealth-scholarships-for-developing-commonwealth-countries/ · https://www.sussex.ac.uk/study/fees-funding/phd-funding/view/1957-Commonwealth-PhD-Scholarships · Official: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk

### 3.5 Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships 2027–28 — 🔴 (see date conflict) ✅
- **Categories for Pakistan:** 1-year Research Fellowship (doctoral candidates) and 3-year PhD Scholarship.
- **Deadline:** **29 Sept 2026** (Swiss country list, per aggregator) vs **31 Oct 2026** (HEC notice). Plan for the earlier one.
- **Funding:** CHF 2,450/month plus health insurance (eligible cases), one-time housing allowance, Swiss Half-Fare card, airfare for eligible non-EU scholars.
- **Must-have:** A Swiss academic supervisor's support letter. Application is free — Swiss authorities warn against fake scholarship offers.
- **Sources:** https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/SGES.aspx · https://bloompakistan.com/switzerland-opens-fully-funded-2027-28-scholarships-for-pakistani-students/ · Portal: https://www.go.eskas.ch/LoginServlet

### 3.6 DAAD EPOS — Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (Germany) — 🟢 course-specific ✅
- **Who:** Graduates from developing/newly industrialised countries with **≥2 years of professional experience** after their bachelor's.
- **Deadlines:** No single deadline — each course on the official 2027/28 list sets its own (example: Freiburg MEG closes 15 Oct 2026). Apply **to the course**, not to DAAD. Up to three courses, in priority order.
- **Funding:** €992/month (master's) or €1,400/month (doctoral), insurance, travel allowance.
- **Sources:** https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50076777 · https://truescho.com/en/blog/daad-epos-scholarship-germany-2027-2028

## 10. 🛡️ SCAM & SAFETY CHECKLIST

- Genuine government scholarships (Chevening, Swiss, Commonwealth, DAAD, OIST PhD fellowship) **never ask for "processing fees"** to agents. Swiss authorities specifically warn about fake offers.
- Only apply through the **official portal** listed on the program's own domain (.gov, .ac.uk, .edu, .ch, .jp, or the organization's known site).
- Social-media posts (LinkedIn/Instagram) are leads, not sources — verify the professor's lab page or university job listing before sending documents.
- Don't share passport scans, CNIC, or bank details with anyone who contacted you first.
- Convert every deadline to your local time zone; UTC/PT/AoE deadlines often fall on a different calendar day in Pakistan.
